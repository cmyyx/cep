Battle Skill
Kay leaps into mid-air for a short power-up, salvages all her Weapons, then unleashes a powerful ground bash with her heavy hammer. The bash deals Nature DMG (based on her Hot Edgy Naives stacks), applies Nature Infliction on enemies within the area of effect, and consumes all Hot Edgy Naives stacks. If Kay consumes 3 Hot Edgy Naives stacks, she also forcibly applies Combustion on enemies. If ＞ 3 Hot Edgy Naives stacks are consumed, the effects will be the same as those for 3 stacks.

Bottomless Arsenal

Breakthrough Stage 1
Kay starts with 3 Weapons. When the battle ends, Kay salvages all thrown Weapons. Salvaging a Weapon grants Kay 1 stack of Hot Edgy Naives and grants the entire team Arts DMG Dealt +1%, with Nature DMG Dealt getting another +1%. Duration: 15s. Max stacks for this effect: 3.

Bottomless Arsenal

Breakthrough Stage 3
Kay starts with 3 Weapons. When the battle ends, Kay salvages all thrown Weapons. Salvaging a Weapon grants Kay 1 stack of Hot Edgy Naives and grants the entire team Arts DMG Dealt +1.5%, with Nature DMG Dealt getting another +1.5%. Duration: 15s. Max stacks for this effect: 3.
